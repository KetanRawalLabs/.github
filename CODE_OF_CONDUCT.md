# Code of Conduct

KetanRawalLabs projects welcome participation from people with different backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and viewpoints. Everyone taking part in issues, discussions, pull requests, and project spaces is expected to help keep them respectful and constructive.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and respectful.
- Discuss ideas and evidence; do not attack people.
- Give and receive feedback professionally.
- Respect privacy and do not publish someone else’s personal information without permission.
- Focus criticism on work, not identity or personal characteristics.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information are not acceptable. Threats or encouragement of harm are not acceptable.

## Reporting and response

Report conduct that violates this policy privately to the repository maintainers through the KetanRawalLabs organization profile. Do not use a public issue for sensitive reports. Maintainers will review reports, protect confidentiality as practical, and take an action proportionate to the situation. Responses may include a warning, removal of content, temporary limits, or exclusion from a project space.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone represents a KetanRawalLabs project in public. Project-specific rules may add detail but should not weaken these expectations.
